PSSMS ERD (text-based)

Entities & attributes
1) ParkingSlot
   - SlotNumber (PK)
   - SlotStatus

2) Car
   - PlateNumber (PK)
   - DriverName
   - PhoneNumber

3) ParkingRecord
   - ParkingRecordID (PK, auto)
   - SlotNumber (FK -> ParkingSlot.SlotNumber)
   - PlateNumber (FK -> Car.PlateNumber)
   - EntryTime
   - ExitTime
   - Duration (derived, stored)

4) Payment
   - PaymentID (PK, auto)
   - ParkingRecordID (FK -> ParkingRecord.ParkingRecordID)
   - AmountPaid
   - PaymentDate

Relationship & cardinality
- ParkingSlot 1  ——  N ParkingRecord
  (One slot can be used by many parking records over time.)

- Car 1 —— N ParkingRecord
  (One car can have many parking records over time.)

- ParkingRecord 1 —— 1 Payment
  (A parking record has at most one payment; payment references the record.)

Keys
- ParkingSlot.SlotNumber -> ParkingRecord.SlotNumber
- Car.PlateNumber -> ParkingRecord.PlateNumber
- ParkingRecord.ParkingRecordID -> Payment.ParkingRecordID
